Mumbai — Fort District
CENTURY-OLD SANCTUARY

The Whispering Chai Counter of Fort

Behind an unmarked wooden swinging door lies a 94-year-old Zoroastrian bakery where the sourdough bun maska recipe hasn't changed since 1932. No Wi-Fi, no loud playlists—just antique mirrors and the ticking of a grandfather clock.

Pune — Kasba Peth
POTTERY MEWS

The 4th Generation Terracotta Mews

Tucked deep into an alleyway where three generations still fire red clay vessels using reclaimed rainwater and kiln wood.

SPICE ALCHEMY

The Secret Rooftop over the Spice Exchange

Ascend 70 narrow stone steps through bags of cardamom and wild turmeric to find an open sky terrace where chai is brewed with heirloom saffron.
